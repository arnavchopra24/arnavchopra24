About

{{ site.author.name }}

A first-year student learning how deals work by writing them down.

I'm a freshman at the Gies College of Business at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, studying Accounting, Finance and Data Science (Class of 2030), and a consultant with Illinois Business Consulting.

I started The Fairness Opinion to teach myself how deals actually work. The name comes from the letter a bank writes to a company's board saying whether the price in a deal is fair to shareholders. Each week I pick one deal, read the press release, investor presentation and filings, and try to answer that same question.

Every piece ends with my verdict: a good deal, overpaid, or too early to tell. I'm still learning, so some of my calls will be wrong. I come back to each one once the deal plays out and track the results on the scorecard, including the misses. Every number comes from a listed source, and when I make an estimate of my own, I label it.
