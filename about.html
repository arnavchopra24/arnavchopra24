{{ site.author.name }}

Learning how deals work by writing them down, one a week.

I'm a sophomore at the Gies College of Business at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, studying Accounting, Finance and Data Science (Class of 2029), and a consultant with Illinois Business Consulting.

I started this journal to learn how deals actually work: what the buyer is really paying, how it is financed, and what has to go right for it to pay off. Each week I take one announced transaction, work through the press release, investor presentation and filings, and write down my view.

Every entry follows the same structure (snapshot, valuation, strategic rationale, financing, synergies or recoveries, risks, market reaction, and my take) and ends with a verdict: a good deal, overpaid, or too early to tell. I revisit each verdict once the deal plays out and keep the results on the scorecard, including the ones I got wrong. Where a company does not disclose a figure, I say so rather than estimate quietly.
