## Deal Snapshot

Who is buying whom, for how much, and how it is paid for. Two or three sentences.

## Valuation

The multiple paid and the premium, and how they compare with two or three similar deals. Say what you think the buyer is really paying for.

## Strategic Rationale

Why the buyer did this, in your own words. Three points at most.

## Financing

Cash, stock or debt, and what the buyer's balance sheet looks like afterwards.

## Synergies

What management promised, how much of it is cost versus revenue, and whether it is believable.

## Risks

Antitrust or regulatory review, integration, shareholder votes, financing.

## Market Reaction

How the buyer's and target's shares moved on announcement day, and what that tells you.

## My Take

Good deal, overpaid, or too early to tell, and why. This is the most important section.

## 30-Second Version

The answer you would give if an interviewer said "walk me through a recent deal."
